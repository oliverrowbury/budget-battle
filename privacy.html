Privacy

The short version: there's no account, no ads, and nothing sold. Here's exactly what's stored and why.

No login, no account

BidOff doesn't have accounts or passwords. The first time you open it, your browser generates a random ID and saves it in localStorage on your own device. That ID is how the game recognizes "this is still you" if you refresh the page — it isn't tied to your name, email, or anything else, and it never leaves your device except to say "this player's turn belongs to this ID" inside the room you're playing in.

What a room stores

While a game is live, its room holds:

  • The display name you type in (anyone can type anything — it's not verified)
  • The bids, picks, and roster your name ends up with
  • Any messages sent in that room's live chat, visible to everyone else in the room

This is all stored in a Firestore database and is readable by anyone who has (or guesses) the room code — the same way anyone with a Google Doc's link can open it. There's no way to browse other people's rooms without their code.

Chat

Chat messages are auto-censored for common profanity before they're sent, but this is a simple word filter, not moderation — don't post anything you wouldn't want the other people in your room to see.

How long it sticks around

A finished or abandoned room is automatically deleted a few days after the last activity in it. Nothing is kept indefinitely.

What's not collected

No analytics trackers, no ad networks, no cookies beyond what's needed to run the game, no selling or sharing of anything with third parties.
